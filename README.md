Mizukaeru is a cute frog completely covered in leaves, with magical gloves made of living water.

The name is a coined Japanese-style compound: mizu means water and kaeru means frog. It is a character name, not a claim that Mizukaeru is a standard Japanese word.

## Install

Keep pet.json and spritesheet.webp together in a folder named mizukaeru.

Windows:

    %USERPROFILE%\.codex\pets\mizukaeru\

macOS/Linux:

    ~/.codex/pets/mizukaeru/

Then restart Codex, or open Settings -> Pets -> Refresh, and select Mizukaeru.

If CODEX_HOME is configured, use <CODEX_HOME>/pets/mizukaeru/ instead.

## Package

- pet.json - v2 pet manifest.
- spritesheet.webp - 8 x 11 transparent animation atlas, 1536 x 2288 pixels.

Preview images and QA notes are included for inspection only; Codex needs the two files above.

## Backstory

Most frogs wait for rain. Mizukaeru learned to wear it.

During the longest dry season, a storm finally broke over the old canopy. A drop of moonlit water landed on Mizukaeru's outstretched hands. It did not fall. It curled around them, becoming two living gloves that moved like small rivers.

The leaves nearby answered the magic, wrapping around the little frog until he looked like a walking piece of the forest. Since then, Mizukaeru has traveled from puddle to puddle, protecting streams, carrying seeds, and guiding lost rainwater back to the roots.

The water gloves are gentle first: they catch, shape, and carry. But when danger comes, Mizukaeru can clap once and call up a bright wall of rain.
